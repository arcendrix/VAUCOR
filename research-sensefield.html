SENSEFIELD.

Perception and mapping

Robotic perception combines cameras and other sensors to estimate a robot's surroundings. SenseField studies localization, mapping and uncertainty rather than claiming autonomous awareness.

ENGINEERING RESEARCH / TECHNICAL BRIEF
01 / HOW IT WORKS

THE
ENGINEERING.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Sensor measurements are timestamped, calibrated and transformed into common coordinate frames. Localization estimates pose; mapping builds a spatial representation; perception identifies obstacles and objects.

APPLICATIONS

Indoor navigation, obstacle awareness, map creation and inspection workflows.

BUILD & VALIDATION

Record representative sensor datasets, calibrate inputs, evaluate pose drift and measure mapping performance in known environments.

ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE

Evidence: sensor calibration, measured localization error, reproducible maps and failure-case reporting.
