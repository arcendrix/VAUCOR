CAREOS.

Service lifecycle software

CareOS is the software direction for recording robotics assets, maintenance plans, service incidents, parts and repair documentation.

ENGINEERING RESEARCH / TECHNICAL BRIEF
01 / HOW IT WORKS

THE
ENGINEERING.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

Each robot has an asset record. Service events link the device, reported symptom, diagnostic findings, parts and work history. Scheduled tasks trigger review rather than silently claiming completion.

APPLICATIONS

Maintenance records, warranty documentation, technician handoffs and lifecycle visibility.

BUILD & VALIDATION

Build an asset register and service-ticket prototype, then validate permissions, event history, exportability and mobile technician workflows.

ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE

Evidence: working asset records, tested maintenance workflow, immutable activity history and documented access controls.
