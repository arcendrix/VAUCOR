Clear answers. No exaggerated claims.

Can I buy a humanoid robot from VAUCOR today?+

VAUCOR is developing its product and service offering. Product availability, pricing and delivery dates have not been announced.

What does VAUCOR actually do?+

Our planned business covers robotics selection, sales, deployment coordination, maintenance and support, subject to supplier partnerships and service readiness.

Where will VAUCOR operate?+

VAUCOR is an emerging Canadian robotics venture. Expansion to other Canadian cities is a long-term objective, not an existing service network.

Are VAUCOR technologies real products?+

The named technologies and divisions on this website are proposed VAUCOR concepts. They are not released products or validated engineering capabilities.
