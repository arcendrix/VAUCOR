01 / TURIONIX CARE

BEYOND
THE SALE.

Our goal: make robot ownership simpler from day one.

01SELECT.Match technology to real needs.

We start with the intended task, operating environment, budget and safety requirements. The evaluation framework considers payload, mobility, battery life, connectivity, integration requirements and vendor support. A recommendation would be made only after checking a specific robot's published capabilities and availability.

PROPOSED PROCESSDiscovery and requirements review
02DEPLOY.Installation planning, setup and guidance.

A deployment plan can cover site readiness, power and charging, network access, safe operating zones, user permissions, operator training and acceptance checks. Any installation work depends on the chosen platform, supplier requirements and TURIONIX's confirmed service capacity.

PROPOSED PROCESSSite readiness and commissioning plan
03MAINTAIN.Build dependable maintenance and support capability.

The proposed maintenance program includes inspection schedules, firmware and software update planning, consumable replacement, battery-health checks, incident records and service-history tracking. Supported products, response times and maintenance contracts have not yet been announced.

PROPOSED PROCESSPreventive maintenance framework
04REPAIR.Diagnostics and repair where manufacturer access permits.

The intended repair workflow begins with fault intake, diagnostic review, parts and warranty checks, a written estimate where applicable, and documented post-repair testing. Manufacturer-authorized repairs will only be offered after the required approvals, training and parts access are secured.

PROPOSED PROCESSRepair and escalation process
