GUARDRAIL.

Robotics safety and permissions

GuardRail is a safety-engineering research track for operating limits, operator approvals, access controls and traceable intervention events.

ENGINEERING RESEARCH / TECHNICAL BRIEF
01 / HOW IT WORKS

THE
ENGINEERING.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

A policy layer defines permitted tasks and operating areas. Safety-critical functions require validated hardware and appropriate standards; software permission checks alone are not a substitute for engineered safeguards.

APPLICATIONS

Role-based operator controls, restricted task zones, intervention logs and reviewable operating procedures.

BUILD & VALIDATION

Create a hazard register, define permission models, test simulated unsafe requests and obtain qualified safety review before physical deployment.

ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE

Evidence: documented hazards, denied unsafe operations, audit records and independent safety assessment for hardware.
