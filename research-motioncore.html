MOTIONCORE.

Motion planning and control

Robotic motion planning is the process of finding collision-aware paths for a robot's joints and end effector. MotionCore is TURIONIX's technical research track for evaluating open robotics motion frameworks.

ENGINEERING RESEARCH / TECHNICAL BRIEF
01 / HOW IT WORKS

THE
ENGINEERING.

TECHNICAL APPROACH

A robot model describes its links, joints and limits. A planner searches for a collision-free configuration path. A controller converts the planned trajectory into commands, subject to hardware and safety constraints.

APPLICATIONS

Manipulator reachability, pick-and-place planning, joint-limit checks and repeatable trajectory validation.

BUILD & VALIDATION

Create a simulated robot model, define planning scenes, benchmark successful plans and document collision-check failures before connecting physical actuators.

ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE

Evidence: reproducible simulation, logged planning results, defined collision boundaries and hardware-specific safety review.
