03 / THE INSPIRATION

JACQUES DE
VAUCANSON.

1709–1782. French inventor. Pioneer of mechanical automata and early industrial automation.

Long before modern robots, Jacques de Vaucanson designed machines that imitated the movements and actions of living creatures. His work challenged what people believed machinery could do.

His mechanical flute player, publicly demonstrated in 1738, used an ingenious system of air, moving fingers and mechanisms to play a real instrument. His celebrated mechanical duck moved, ate and appeared to digest food—although its apparent digestion was an illusion. Vaucanson later turned to automated silk production and developed pioneering textile machinery.

The VAUCOR brand takes inspiration from Vaucanson’s legacy: the ambition to turn extraordinary engineering into machines that move and work in the physical world. VAUCOR is our modern brand name, not his historical name or an acronym.
