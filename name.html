01 / THE NAME

VAUCOR.

A distinctive name for a company focused on making robotics useful beyond the laboratory.

VAUCOR represents a straightforward idea: advanced machines should be practical, supported and ready to work in the real world. Our focus spans humanoid, commercial and residential robotics, from selecting and installing systems to maintaining and repairing them.

03 / THE ORIGIN STORY

INSPIRED BY
THE PIONEERS.

VAUCOR draws inspiration from Jacques de Vaucanson (1709–1782), the French inventor whose mechanical automata helped shape the history of robotics.

In the 1730s, Vaucanson built a remarkable mechanical flute player and an automaton duck. He later advanced automated textile machinery. His work brought the idea of intelligent-seeming machines into physical motion centuries before modern robotics.

The name VAUCOR is a contemporary brand name inspired by this pioneering tradition, not the historical inventor’s actual name or an established acronym.
