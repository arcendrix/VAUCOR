01 / WHAT TURIONIX MEANS

A NAME FOR
FORWARD MOTION.

Turionix is a coined name built to express the future of robotics and automation.

We associate Turionix with three ideas: technology that moves industries forward, engineering that solves practical problems, and reliable support that keeps machines working. It is not an acronym or a historical surname. It is our own name for a company committed to making advanced robotics useful, accessible and dependable.

03 / WHO WE ARE

TECHNOLOGY IS ONLY
THE BEGINNING.

Turionix is focused on the practical side of robotics and automation: sales, integration, preventive maintenance, diagnostics, repair and training.
